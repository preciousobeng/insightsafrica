Administrative area identifiers — version 2

This release corrects administrative names in rainfall data, boundaries and derived products. It deliberately changes affected public identifiers; update saved queries and cached extracts together.

  • Top-level identifiers are Name. Cape Verde, Côte d’Ivoire and South Africa no longer use |Name.
  • Child identifiers are Name|Parent. Existing Ghana, Nigeria and Senegal names retain this convention.
  • Côte d’Ivoire’s second level contains 31 regions and two autonomous districts: 33 distinctly named features. GeoJSON admin_type distinguishes them.
  • The old 14 region entries were child statistics overwritten under parent names. They cannot be treated as district totals or mapped one-to-one to the corrected 33 entries. Re-download regional history and its derived products.

Applies to rainfall layer JSON, CSV downloads (including the archive), administrative GeoJSON, anomaly and SPI responses, and affected derived risk/outlook products. API responses for flood and boundary routes identify this release with X-Administrative-Key-Version: 2. The CSV column layout and JSON nesting remain unchanged.

The frontend is updated with the data. Ghana URLs and redirects are retained. Rebuild local Databricks extracts and joins from corrected data rather than reusing previously exported rows.

Existing sampling-method transition

For Nigeria, Côte d’Ivoire, Senegal, Cape Verde and South Africa, the production archive uses pixel-centre sampling (all_touched=False) through April 2026 and all-intersecting-pixel sampling (all_touched=True) from May 2026. Ghana uses the latter throughout the audited archive. Trends and anomalies spanning this transition combine two sampling methods. This identifier repair preserves each file’s original method and stored values outside the corrected Côte d’Ivoire second level; it does not align sampling methods. A separate methodology release is required for that change.
